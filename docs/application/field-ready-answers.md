## Product & Traction

**What is the problem you are solving? (2-3 sentences)**
Software agencies coordinate client funding, milestone approval and contractor payments in separate systems. That leaves uncertainty over whether work and scope changes are actually funded, while public stablecoin payouts can expose individual rates. The initial customer is an agency already receiving and paying USDC.

**Why are you working on this idea? (2-3 sentences)**
We want stablecoins to support the commercial agreement around software work, not just the final transfer. Funding certainty and confidential rates need to work together for agencies and their contractors. Any personal motivation or firsthand customer story must be added only if factually accurate for the founder.

**Unique insight or advantage (2-3 sentences)**
The agency is the entry point: it brings an existing client and contractor team rather than requiring a new marketplace. Our hypothesis is that committed private allocations and funded scope changes become more useful when tied to the same versioned milestone agreement. Payment approvals and private payroll already exist; we are validating the agency-specific combination rather than claiming those primitives are new.

## Why Base

**Why do you want to join Base Batches? (1-2 sentences)**
We want to use the program to turn a focused agency-payments hypothesis into a verified workflow and a real pilot. Base’s payment ecosystem, technical feedback and founder support are relevant to proving confidential settlement and testing customer demand.

**What is or will be onchain? What uses Base? (2-3 sentences)**
Base is our intended default network for contract-held funds, milestone authorization, confidential release and refund. Scope and document commitments would bind the agreement, while encrypted documents and application records stay offchain with role-specific access. We are testing Inco interoperability on Base Sepolia before enabling payments.
