# Founder video: product segment

The official application requires a 1-5 minute non-public founding-team video made specifically for Base Batches, covering team, company and problem. This is the product segment; add the real founder introduction. A real founder must record it; synthetic narration must not pretend to be a founder appearance.

“We’re building Scopeveil, private project payments for software agencies on Base.

An agency needs to know the client’s money is reserved before work starts. Its contractors need confidence in their agreed share. But public stablecoin payments can expose everyone’s rates, and a change in scope can leave everyone arguing about what was funded.

Scopeveil brings that into one agreement. The client reserves funds. The agency delivers a milestone. The client approves that exact version, and the agreed shares go to the agency and contractors. Extra scope requires fresh approval and funding.

We’re at concept stage. We’re validating the agency workflow and testing the hardest dependency first: confidential escrow on Base Sepolia. We won’t call a successful transfer a safe escrow product.

Our goal for the program is a verified end-to-end workflow and an agency pilot, with evidence that it reduces payment coordination and reconciliation work.”
