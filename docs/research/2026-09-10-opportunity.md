# Opportunity research
Checked 2026-09-10. These are source-backed observations, not acceptance predictions.

- https://www.base.org/batches : Base-first, pre-product to post-MVP, no formal Seed; Sep 10 11:59PM PT; Sep 17 acceptances; Sep21-Nov15 virtual; Nov17 New York demo day.
- https://blog.base.org/introducing-base-batches-004 : $100K investment offer subject to diligence. Not a grant or prize.
- https://www.base.org/batches/apply : written and video submission; no draft saving.
- https://docs.inco.org/ctoken/overview : Base/Base Sepolia cTokens, shield/send/unshield/reveal. Circle Base Sepolia USDC 0x036CbD53842c5426634e7929541eC2318f3dCF7e. Contract-controlled escrow not proven by wallet flow.
- https://docs.inco.org/ctoken/raw : wrap(address,uint256), encrypted confidentialTransfer, withdrawal attestations, covalidator trust/ACL. Wrapping/unwrapping exposes ordinary ERC20 operations. Encrypted balance sufficiency is not established by a successful receipt alone.
- https://www.request.finance/ : invoice approvals, contractor/batch payments already offered. Do not claim novelty for these primitives.
- https://www.toku.com/resources/how-toku-runs-fully-private-stablecoin-payroll-on-aleo-and-usad : vendor describes production private stablecoin payroll. Differentiate funded agency engagements, committed private allocations and funded scope changes, not privacy alone.

Hypotheses to validate: agency pain from unfunded work, contractor-rate disclosure and scope creep; willingness to fund full engagements upfront; preferred dispute reviewer; willingness to pay for coordination rather than simple transfers. Interview three agencies and request one actual pilot commitment. Subscription vs transaction fee is an experiment, not published pricing.
